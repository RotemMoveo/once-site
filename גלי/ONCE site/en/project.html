About the project
A unique, first-of-its-kind project in northern Rehovot, designed by the Etgar Nol Architects firm, offering an innovative living concept across two 10-storey buildings (3–4 room apartments, garden apartments and penthouses). The project combines shared workspaces and a meeting room — removing the need for a study inside the apartment — alongside a shared, open garden patio for leisure hours.
